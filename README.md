# CodeAlpha_Phishing_Awareness
## 🎯 Features
* **Phishing Education:** Covers spear phishing, whaling, and pretexting.
* **Red Flag Identification:** Teaches how to spot fake domains, the HTTPS myth, and malicious emails.
* **Interactive HTML Quiz:** A dynamic JavaScript-based quiz that evaluates user knowledge and provides immediate scoring and feedback.

# Professional PPT Structure
Slide 1: Title Slide

Title: Phishing Awareness & Defense Training

Subtitle: CodeAlpha Cyber Security Internship - Task 2

Footer: Presented by Vishal | CodeAlpha

Slide 2: What is Phishing?

Bullet 1: A cyber attack where threat actors impersonate legitimate entities.

Bullet 2: Goal: Steal credentials, financial data, or deploy malware.

Bullet 3: The Human Firewall: Why end-user awareness is the ultimate line of defense.

Slide 3: The Anatomy of a Phishing Email

Visual: Add a screenshot of a fake email with arrows pointing to red flags.

Bullet Points: Urgent/threatening language, mismatched sender domains, generic greetings, suspicious attachments.

Slide 4: Spotting Fake Websites

Bullet 1: Typosquatting (e.g., rnicrosoft.com instead of microsoft.com).

Bullet 2: The HTTPS Myth: A padlock does not mean the site is safe; it only means the connection is encrypted.

Bullet 3: Aggressive pop-ups and broken secondary links.

Slide 5: Social Engineering Tactics

Spear Phishing: Highly targeted attacks using personalized info.

Whaling: Targeting high-profile executives (CEOs, CFOs).

Pretexting: Fabricating a scenario (e.g., fake IT support).

Slide 6: Real-World Example

Scenario: Business Email Compromise (BEC).

Detail: An attacker spoofs the CEO's email and requests an urgent, confidential wire transfer from the finance department, bypassing standard verification.

Slide 7: Best Practices & Defense

Bullet 1: Always verify independently (navigate directly to the website, do not click links).

Bullet 2: Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) everywhere.

Bullet 3: Use a Password Manager (they won't autofill on fake domains).

Slide 8: Interactive Training Module

Text: Let's test your knowledge!
